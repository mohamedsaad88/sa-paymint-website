## Contact behavior

The form validates and prepares email locally in the browser. It sends nothing to a server, writes no browser storage and uses no webhook. Visitors must review and send via their email client. Controls are disabled before hydration; a direct-email option is supplied without JavaScript. This is actual website behavior, not a claim about PayMint’s financial products.
